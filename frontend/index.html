HOW IT'S PROTECTED

A vault that even a breach can't open

Three layers stand between an attacker and your data — and none of them rely on trusting us.

01

Local key derivation

Your master password derives a local encryption key — it's never transmitted or stored anywhere.

02

Encrypted in-browser

Every credential is encrypted with that key before the request ever leaves your browser.

03

KeyZentext-only storage

The database only ever stores KeyZentext — an attacker who reaches it finds nothing readable.
